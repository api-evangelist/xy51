# Xiao-Y Technology (小y科技 / xy51)

> Xiao-Y Technology (小y科技), operated by Beijing Shiyou Interactive Technology Co., Ltd. (北京视游互动科技有限公司), is a Chinese smart-TV and big-screen gaming platform. It runs the 小y游戏厅 TV game store and YFans app, publishes licensed TV editions of titles such as 葫芦娃, 胡莱三国2, 我叫MT2, and 中超荣耀, and operates the 小y手游开放平台 — a developer open platform where game studios integrate via the first-party YTS SDKs (Android Fusion + iOS) to distribute games across smart TVs with remote, gamepad, and phone control. Backed by Qiming Venture Partners. There is no public REST API product; platform integration is SDK-based behind developer registration.
